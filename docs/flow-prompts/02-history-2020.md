## Main · Two bars, light travelling

```
Vertical macro cinematography of two identical plain gold bars with completely smooth, blank, unstamped faces lying side by side on dark charcoal fabric, the left bar in deep shadow and the right bar fully lit. Across the duration the warm tungsten key light at 3200K travels slowly and smoothly from right to left through the frame, so the left bar gradually comes up into full light while the right falls away into shadow, the handover happening around the midpoint of the shot. Camera is locked on a tripod, completely static, no push and no handheld drift. Shot on a 100mm macro lens at f/2.8, both bars held in acceptable focus, the fabric texture softening at the edges of frame. A dim cool fill at 5600K from low front at one-eighth intensity keeps the shadowed bar readable rather than black. Deep vignette, pure near-black unlit background with no visible horizon. Colour palette strictly limited to warm gold, amber and charcoal. Fine film grain, photorealistic, deliberate, editorial product photography. Upper half of the vertical frame stays empty and dark throughout for a text overlay. One continuous take, no cuts.
```

Deep vignette, near-black unlit background, colour palette limited to warm gold, amber and charcoal. Fine film grain, photorealistic, unhurried, editorial.
