## Main · Calibration weights

```
Vertical macro cinematography looking straight down from directly overhead at a row of plain polished gold cylinders of decreasing size, completely smooth and unmarked, arranged in a neat row on rough black volcanic stone, largest to smallest, perfectly aligned along a single axis. Nothing in the frame moves. Across the duration a broad soft warm key light at 3400K drifts slowly from the upper left toward directly overhead, so the shadow cast by each cylinder shortens smoothly and evenly as the light rises. Camera is locked off in a true overhead position and does not move at all, no push and no drift. Shot on a 100mm macro lens at f/4, the top faces of the cylinders sharp and the stone falling gently soft toward the frame edges. A weak cool fill at 5600K from the lower right at one-tenth intensity keeps the shadowed faces readable. The volcanic stone is matte, deeply textured, near-black, and absorbs light almost completely. Deep vignette at the frame edges. Colour palette strictly limited to warm gold, amber and near-black. Fine film grain, photorealistic, quiet, precise, patient, editorial still-life quality. Upper half of the vertical frame stays empty and dark throughout for a text overlay. One continuous take, no cuts.
```
