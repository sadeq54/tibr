# What the Arabic gold accounts actually do

Field notes from Instagram search, 29 Aug 2026. Every number here was read off
a live post, not estimated.

## The accounts

**aldehdar.jewellry** — 145K followers, 15,214 posts, Jordanian jeweller since
1989. Ranks first for "سعر الذهب اليوم".

Their winning format is a **shaky phone video of the shop's own price board**,
with a hand holding a PAMP bar in shot, and a handwritten-style text sticker
reading "انخفاض في أسعار الذهب الآن 🔥". 92 likes, 2 comments, 4 shares in 19
hours. The comments are a real conversation — "كيف اغتنم الفرصه قبل الانخفاض 😂",
"معقول" — not emoji spam.

Their whole grid is real product: hands holding bars, necklaces on stands, the
shop front. Seven highlights, all merchandise categories.

**alfarooo8** — 571K followers, 70,100 posts, Bahraini general-interest account.
Gold price is one of many topics.

A static image did **1.1K likes and 41 comments**. Bright red and white, not
gold-on-black. A photo of real jewellery cupped in a hand. Then a table with two
price columns — **سعر الشراء** and **سعر البيع** — split further into **جديد**
and **مستعمل**. A timestamp, "01:50 مساءً". The caption carries a disclaimer:
"الأسعار المعروضة في البوست هي أسعار تقريبية لمتوسط السوق، وقد تختلف الأسعار
الحقيقية بحسب التاجر أو المصنعية".

Note the rate, though: 1.1K on 571K followers is 0.19%. High absolute numbers,
weak engagement. Do not copy their posting volume; copy the card.

**Roya / المملكة** — news outlet. Reel: photo of gold bangles, one enormous
number, and the words "في انخفاض ملموس".

## What every one of them does that we do not

**Two prices, not one.** Buy and sell — سعر الشراء and سعر البيع. This is the
biggest single gap. A reader wants to know what they will pay and what they
would get back. We publish one number, which answers neither question.

**New versus used.** alfarooo8 splits جديد from مستعمل. The spread between them
is what a customer actually loses on resale.

**Real gold, real hands, real shops.** Photographs, not renders. Ours is the
only abstract card in the results.

**Bright colour.** Red and white, blue and white. Our dark gold on near-black is
handsome in isolation and invisible in a scrolling feed of white cards.

**A timestamp.** "01:50 مساءً" says this is fresh right now. Our date says today
but not when.

**The move in words first.** "هبوط في أسعار الذهب", "في انخفاض ملموس". They lead
with what happened; we lead with a figure and let the reader work it out.

**A visible human.** A hand at minimum. There is no person anywhere in our feed.

**An honest disclaimer.** alfarooo8 says the prices are approximate and vary by
dealer and making charge. It costs nothing and buys credibility — and it is
true of our numbers too.

## What we do better

Coverage. 53 countries against their one. Nobody else answers "what is gold
worth in my country today" for a Syrian or a Moroccan reader. That is the
position worth owning, and none of the accounts above compete for it.

## Search behaviour

Searching "اسعار الذهب في السعودية" returns **reels in every top slot**. For
country-specific queries the format that surfaces is video, not the carousel.
